## Intent

Companion promo to the pairing ad. The pairing promo answers "how do I connect?";
this cut answers "what can I do once connected?"

Audience: developers running Claude Code or Codex locally. Tone: terminal-native,
confident, useful. Silent-first: every claim is burned into the frames and the
three beats remain legible without audio.

## Assets

- `assets/terminal.png` — real conversation and terminal viewer capture.
- `assets/search.png` — real full-text search capture.

## Customizations

- Keep the phone-device treatment and screen-specific motion from the source promo.
- Show terminal visibility first, prompt queueing second, and searchable history third.
- Keep one product action per frame and settle each frame before the cut.
- End on a concise product promise rather than repeating the pairing CTA.
- Include the local promo's reusable BGM/SFX mix as an optional audio export; the
	visual cut must remain fully legible and usable muted.

## Relationship to the pairing promo

This is intentionally a separate promo. Do not add these beats to the setup-friction
cut: pairing and product capability are different arguments with different pacing.
